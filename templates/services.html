Quality Materials

The Wood We Use

Great furniture starts with great wood. We only use properly dried and seasoned hardwoods that resist termites, moisture, and bending.

Sagwan (Teak Wood)

Best for Furniture · High Durability

Naturally water and termite resistant with rich natural oils. Strong, long-lasting, and ideal for dining tables, beds, and main entrance doors.

  • Lasts 50+ Years
  • No Bending or Cracking
  • Beautiful Golden Brown Color

Sheesham (Indian Rosewood)

Traditional Saharanpur Hardwood

Heavy, dense, and durable with dark chocolate streaks. Takes polish very well and is famous for solid wood furniture and dining sets.

  • Very Heavy and Sturdy
  • Rich Natural Grain Patterns
  • Great for Dining & Beds

White Oak & Walnut

Modern Imported Hardwoods

Clean, straight-grain woods with contemporary shades. Perfect for modern villas, minimalist wall paneling, and designer office furniture.

  • Modern, Elegant Look
  • Strong and Scratch Resistant
  • Beautiful Natural Matt Finish

HDHMR & Waterproof Plywood

Waterproof & Termite Resistant

Specially used for modular kitchens, bathroom vanities, and wardrobes where water protection is essential.

  • 100% Waterproof (BWP 710)
  • Strong Screw Holding
  • Termite & Fungus Proof

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about materials, timelines, delivery, and warranty.

Timelines depend on the project size. A single dining table or bed usually takes 2 to 3 weeks. A complete modular kitchen or wardrobe set takes 3 to 4 weeks. We always give you a confirmed delivery date before starting.

Most factory furniture uses particle board or cheap MDF that absorbs moisture and breaks within a few years. We use 100% solid seasoned hardwood (Teak, Sheesham) and waterproof marine ply that lasts for decades.

Yes, everything is made to order. You can choose the exact size, wood type (Teak, Sheesham, Oak, Walnut), hardware brands (Blum, Hafele, Hettich), and finish (glossy, matt, or natural polish).

All our wood is treated against termites and borer insects. We also dry the wood properly before crafting to prevent cracks, warping, and bending in any season.
